It Happened Again..

By decoy

Expose: Rehab systems don't work as punishment of violent crimes.

In this video, decoy shows us an example where a rehab system of a violent criminal in Chicago doesn't work. A arsonist with over 70 previous arrests has only lead to 2 years probation. 
I wondered of the bat why this is? what are the politicians saying to justify this? And more importantly, what is the real reason. I speculate that they say its for humanitarian purposes to keep him out of jail, but in reality its probably because its more expensive in the short term to put him in jail. But its anyone's guess. 
The Chicago governor tried to blame Trump and ICE for the behavior, even though his state departments are responsible over such crimes. My question, even if ICE somehow distracts officers from going into the streets and protecting citizens, how come the criminal can be arrested OVER 70 TIMES without being put in jail? At some point, that's not because of Trump...
decoy also claimed that its harder to report crimes in cities run by democrats, because its a cheep way to reduce or "juke" negative crime statistics. I think there may lay some truth to that, because everyone knows having more administration automated or pushed on individuals generally leads to worse help or reporting, but id rather in that case argue its because of incompetence and not malicious behavior. Just my thought. 

I think this is a directed, but truthful video showing a concrete example of how rehabbing people docent always work as punishment. But you'd need more than one example to prove anything. Thing is, this channel and many others constantly expose this type of behavior all the time, and together they make you wonder.
