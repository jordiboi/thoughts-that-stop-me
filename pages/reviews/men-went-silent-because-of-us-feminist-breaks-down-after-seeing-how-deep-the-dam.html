"Men Went Silent Because Of US" - Feminist BREAKS Down After Seeing How Deep The Damage Goes

By Greentext Gems

A short story about how a feminist starts criticizing her own ideas.

This format of video is in retrospect pretty stupid, and its obviously not a true story. But I still enjoyed it the first time I listened to it. Even if the subject matter is about how "feminism=bad", it still reminds me that we can all fall into a trap of thinking we are right in our believes. This is my greatest take away from listening:
'Just because you can intellectualize something, that does not mean an idea is write or even need to be intellectualized in the first place'. 
I am critical to feminism, but i have previously also called myself one. I think this is why I related so strongly to the video at the time. Now days I want to take the route that I am a non-resistant non-believer to feminism, and I have promised myself to always stay open minded to the idea. But no matter what we call ourselves, we must not forget that sometimes we use different definitions for the the same words, and we are often way more alike each other than we think, despite the language barrier.
