Reaction: Department of education shut down by Trump.

Department of education shut down; good or bad?

Asmongolds initial reaction is that this is a positive change, but he does mention that if he was Trump he would instead reform the school system rather than shut it down. The reasoning is because "The children are retarded" (paraphrasing). it isn't working anyway, therefore it is a waste of money. I didn't immediately disagree with this statement, however, I have a alternative take on what's going on.
Firstly id like to point out that if in fact the department does nothing for children, then it is a waste of money. The proposition is obviously true, but that does not mean we have the whole picture of reality. 
In my opinion, the department of education is technically a waste of money at the moment, because it is overshadowed by something more influential that affects children's intelligence, namely social media. 
I imagine if trump would have shut down social media instead of the department, then results would have gone up instead. Now lets say for argument sake, Trump would have shut down both at once, then i actually think test results would have gone up, but not as much as they could have with a department of education still running. 
These were just my immediate thought on the subject and I don't have any statistics to support my claim, but it just seems more reasonable to first shut down social media before a state department. 
So why did Trump do this rather than that? I think he's just using low results as an excuse to privatize education, and I'm pretty sure that test results wont go up at all because of this decision, but he is saving money for the state while at the same time making people in charge of privatized education richer. 

When it comes to Zack's take on what the system might look like to sort children, i don't think its especially spicy. Germany already kind of has this sort of system, besides whatever educational system we have in school, I just think its more important having more freedom as an adult in any case. Lets be honest about one thing in all cases; All school systems are already forcing kids to being a certain way, and whatever system we have in place will manipulate them into agreeing with certain values no matter what. As long as the system tries benefiting the individual in the long run then that is what's most important. 
I don't know if I agree with his take on preparing kids for specific jobs.

  • He believes reforming the system would be a better option.
  • You can generally not educate everyone to becoming intellectual, so it would be better for schools to take "lost causes" and push them towards less intellectual trade skills.  
  • "People who cheat through school are a waste of tax money".
  • "If you removed the bottom 5% of dumb, annoying, misbehaving, problematic kids in your school, what percentage of problems in your school would have gone away? 100%".
